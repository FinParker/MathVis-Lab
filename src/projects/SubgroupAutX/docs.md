# Aut(X) Subgroups (S3 Group)

For a set $X=\{1,2,3\}$, $Aut(X)$ is the group of all bijections (permutations) from $X$ to $X$, commonly known as the **Symmetric Group $S_3$**.

The size of this group is $3! = 6$. Let's list these 6 elements using permutation notation:

*   $e$: Identity $(1, 2, 3) \to (1, 2, 3)$
*   $(12)$: Swap 1 and 2, keep 3 fixed
*   $(23)$: Swap 2 and 3, keep 1 fixed
*   $(13)$: Swap 1 and 3, keep 2 fixed
*   $(123)$: 1 to 2, 2 to 3, 3 to 1 (Cyclic shift left)
*   $(132)$: 1 to 3, 3 to 2, 2 to 1 (Cyclic shift right)

By **Lagrange's Theorem**, the size of any subgroup must divide the order of the group (6). Thus, possible subgroup sizes are **1, 2, 3, and 6**.

## List of All Subgroups

### 1. Trivial Subgroup (Size 1)
*   $E = \{e\}$

### 2. Order 2 Subgroups (Reflections)
Generated by a single swap (involution).
*   $H_1 = \{e, (12)\}$
*   $H_2 = \{e, (23)\}$
*   $H_3 = \{e, (13)\}$

### 3. Order 3 Subgroup (Rotations)
Generated by cyclic permutations. Also known as the **Alternating Group $A_3$**.
*   $H_4 = \{e, (123), (132)\}$

### 4. Full Group (Size 6)
*   $S_3$: All 6 elements.

## Subgroup Lattice

We can visualize the hierarchy using a **Subgroup Lattice (Hasse Diagram)**:
*   **Top**: $S_3$
*   **Middle**: $H_4$ (Order 3) alongside $H_1, H_2, H_3$ (Order 2)
*   **Bottom**: $\{e\}$

**Key Insight**: $S_3$ is the smallest **non-abelian group**.
For example, $(12) \circ (23) = (123)$, but $(23) \circ (12) = (132)$. Order matters!
